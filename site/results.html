Key Findings

  • The unvaccinated Omicron-infected group shows a dramatic upregulation of interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs).
  • Top enriched GO terms are all antiviral: defense response to virus, response to virus, regulation of viral genome replication, antiviral innate immune response.
  • The RIG-I-like receptor, TLR, NOD-like receptor, and cytosolic DNA sensing pathways are all significantly enriched.
  • Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) and Influenza A are the top KEGG pathways, consistent with the viral etiology of the infection.
