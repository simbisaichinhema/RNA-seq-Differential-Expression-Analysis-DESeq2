# Chapter 1: Introduction

## What is RNA-seq?

RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) is a high-throughput sequencing technology that captures the transcriptome — the complete set of RNA transcripts expressed in a cell or tissue at a given moment. Unlike microarrays, RNA-seq does not require pre-designed probes, enabling unbiased detection of novel transcripts, splice variants, and low-abundance transcripts.

## Why Compare Vaccinated vs. Unvaccinated?

SARS-CoV-2 vaccination primes the innate immune system, a phenomenon known as **trained immunity**. In the context of Omicron infection, understanding how prior vaccination reshapes the host transcriptomic response is critical for:

- Evaluating vaccine-induced protection
- Identifying correlates of immune protection
- Understanding differences in disease severity

## The Dataset: GSE201530

GEO accession **GSE201530** — a public transcriptomic dataset containing bulk RNA-seq data from PBMCs of Omicron-infected individuals with varying vaccination and infection histories.

## Project Goal

Identify differentially expressed genes between healthy controls and unvaccinated infected individuals, and characterize the enriched biological processes and pathways.
