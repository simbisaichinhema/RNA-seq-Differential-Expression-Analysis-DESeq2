# Chapter 6: Conclusion

## Summary

This RNA-seq analysis of GSE201530 identified **445 upregulated** and **74 downregulated** genes (padj < 0.05, |log2FC| > 1) when comparing unvaccinated Omicron-infected individuals to healthy controls.

## Key Findings

1. **Strong antiviral transcriptional response** — dominated by interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs) and innate immune signaling
2. **Multiple innate sensing pathways enriched** — RIG-I-like, TLR, NOD-like, and cytosolic DNA sensing pathways
3. **Coronavirus disease and Influenza A** are among the top enriched KEGG pathways
4. **The enrichment results are consistent with known Omicron biology** and published literature

## Limitations

- Observational study — no causal inference
- Bulk RNA-seq — averages across cell types
- Cross-sectional — no temporal dynamics
- No comparison with vaccinated-infected group
